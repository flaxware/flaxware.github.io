    Why it's easy to focus here

    One thing at a time

    The search box is the first thing you see. Nothing moves, blinks, or competes for your attention.

    Your engine, remembered

    Choose an engine once and FlaxGo remembers it for your next visit.

    Clear, readable text

    Large type, generous spacing, and high-contrast colours make reading less tiring.

    Quick start

    1. Click the search box, or press /.
    2. Type your question and press Enter.
    3. To change engines, click the small triangle and pick one from the list.
    4. Press Esc to close the list without changing anything.
